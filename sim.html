zen welcome, simulated

The real app runs in the phone on the left, in iPhone mode: it photographs the zen card, tracks it, and opens the table beside it. The camera is faked as a room with a card on a wooden table, and dragging inside the phone tilts it like a real phone.

waiting for the intro

Walk through it

  1. Tap open my welcome, then press look at card below.
  2. Tap the round shutter to photograph the card. The app learns it in a few seconds.
  3. The table opens beside the card. Tap a perk card or a perk button.

Move the phone

Or drag inside the phone, or use the arrow keys. While the perk cards are up, dragging swipes the card belt instead.

Jump around

Perk buttons work once the cards have risen. Sound plays after your first click in the phone. Android's real table tracking (WebXR) can only be checked on a phone.
